Launch odds

Odds = the share of similar protocols that record a token (peers’ record, shrunk toward the overall share). It describes the record; it does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Protocols are grouped by category group (the 6 largest categories, everything else as Other) × TVL band ($5M–$20M, $20M–$100M, $100M–$1B, $1B+). Raw share = trading protocols in the cell / all protocols in the cell. Shrunk odds = (trading in cell + 20 × overall share) / (n in cell + 20), clamped to [0.02, 0.95]. A cell with fewer than 5 protocols falls back to its category-level share, then to the overall share.

Farm method

  • Gas (assumption): Ethereum $8.00 per action, every other chain $0.50 per action. A protocol’s farm chain is the cheapest chain in its chains list under this rule (Ethereum only if it is the only chain listed).
  • Farm cost: 4 weekly actions × gas + first-transaction gas. Mid cost is $2.50 on a $0.50 chain and $40 on Ethereum; low is half of mid, high is double mid.
  • Deposit (assumption): farm-size deposit $150 on cheap chains, $50 on Ethereum (high gas). Farm verdict = full deposit, Thin = half deposit, Skip = $0.
  • Farmer’s share (assumption): a small farmer receives 0.1% of the launch value. Farmer gain = expected value × 0.001.
  • Verdict rule: Farm if farmer gain > 3 × farm cost (mid); Thin if farmer gain > 1 × farm cost; otherwise Skip. Breakeven = farm cost (mid): the airdrop value the farmer must receive to break even.
  • Honesty: “records no token” means the snapshot’s record carries no token for the protocol — it is not proof that no token exists. Odds describe the peers’ record; they do not predict whether or when a token is issued.

Launch Radar ranks protocols that record no token by expected value = model launch value × launch odds. The board’s implied value is this entry’s own claim. Palette: dark slate, light text, sky accent; green = Farm, amber = Thin, grey = Skip.
